About Us

At CARISHMA, we believe true luxury is defined by craftsmanship, precision, and timeless design.

Rooted in Indian heritage and reimagined for the modern gentleman, our collections celebrate impeccable tailoring, refined silhouettes, luxurious fabrics, and intricate handcrafted detailing. Every garment is thoughtfully created to embody understated elegance, offering occasion wear that is both contemporary and enduring.

Designed for the man who values authenticity, sophistication, and quality above trends, CARISHMA is a reflection of quiet confidence, where every stitch tells a story, and every creation is made to be worn with distinction.

The Bespoke Process

From first conversation to final fitting

  1. 01

    Consult

    Share the occasion, the dates and what you have in mind. Over WhatsApp, on a call, or at the studio.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Detailed measurements are taken in person. Fabrics, colours and details are chosen together.

  3. 03

    Fit

    A trial fitting to refine the cut before the final finishing and embroidery.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    The finished garment, ready for the occasion.
